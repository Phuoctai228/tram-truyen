UC ID and Name: UC-26-Unlock Paid Chapter
Created By: TaiTP Date Created: 2026-09-18
Primary Actor: Member Secondary Actors: None
Trigger: Member accesses a locked VIP chapter (which automatically opens the VIP unlock modal) or clicks the "Mở khóa chương" button on the VIP paywall card.
Description: Allows an authenticated Member to spend Coins from their wallet to permanently unlock and read a paid VIP chapter.
Preconditions:

PRE-1. Member is logged into the system.

PRE-2. The target chapter is a published VIP chapter with a positive Coin price.

PRE-3. Member has not previously unlocked this chapter.
Postconditions:

POST-1. The chapter's Coin price is deducted from the Member's wallet balance and recorded in coin_transactions.

POST-2. The Member's chapter activity record is marked as unlocked (isUnlocked = true) with pricePaid and unlockedAt timestamp.

POST-3. The full content of the VIP chapter is displayed to the Member.
Normal Flow:

A. Unlock VIP Chapter Successfully

  1. Member accesses a locked VIP chapter page and opens the VIP unlock modal (or clicks "Mở khóa chương").
  2. The system displays the VIP unlock modal showing the chapter price, the Member's current Coin balance, and the "Mở khóa" button.
  3. Member clicks the "Mở khóa" button to confirm the purchase.
  4. The system validates the chapter's eligibility and acquires a pessimistic write lock on the Member's user record.
  5. The system verifies that the Member has not already unlocked the chapter and that their total Coin balance is sufficient.
  6. The system deducts the chapter price from the Member's wallet balance, records a CHAPTER_UNLOCK transaction, and saves the unlocked status in user_chapter_activities within a single ACID transaction.
  7. The system redirects the Member back to the chapter reading page with a success flash message and renders the full chapter content.
Alternative Flows:

A.2 Insufficient Coin Balance

  1. The system determines the Member's Coin balance is less than the chapter price when rendering the VIP unlock modal.
  2. The system displays the Coin shortfall, suggests recommended top-up packages, and renders the "Nạp Coin Ngay" button instead of the unlock form.
  3. Member clicks "Nạp Coin Ngay" to navigate to the payment top-up page, or clicks "Để sau" to close the modal.

A.3 Cancel Unlock Modal

  1. Member clicks the "Hủy" button or the "close" icon on the VIP unlock modal.
  2. The system closes the modal and keeps the Member on the chapter preview page.
  3. Back to step 1.

A.5 Chapter Already Unlocked

  1. The system detects during transaction processing that the chapter is already unlocked for this Member.
  2. The system skips deducting Coins and redirects the Member directly to the unlocked chapter page.
Exceptions:

EX-1 Session Invalid (Not Logged In)

  1. Guest clicks "Đăng nhập ngay" in the VIP modal or attempts to submit an unlock request without a valid session.
  2. The system redirects the user to the login page.

EX-6 Transaction Failure

  1. A database error or insufficient balance condition occurs during the transactional deduction.
  2. The system rolls back the entire transaction and redirects back to the chapter page with an error flash message.
Priority: High, Must Have
Frequency of Use: High
Business Rules: BR-06, BR-12, BR-20, BR-21
Other Information: The modal also includes an optional checkbox "Tự động mở khóa các chương VIP tiếp theo của tác phẩm này" stored in browser localStorage.
Assumptions:
  • Member has stable internet connection.
