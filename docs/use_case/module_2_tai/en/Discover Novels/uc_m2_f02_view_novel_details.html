UC ID and Name: UC-10-View Novel Details
Created By: TaiTP Date Created: 2026-09-18
Primary Actor: Guest, Member, Staff, Admin Secondary Actors: None
Trigger: Actor clicks on a novel card or title link from the homepage, category list, search results, leaderboard, bookshelf, or reading history.
Description: Allows the actor to view comprehensive details of a specific novel, including its cover image, author, categories, status, total views, favorite count, rating summary, synopsis, full published chapter list (withVIP indicators and sort order toggle), same-author novels, and community reviews/comments.
Preconditions:

PRE-1. The requested novel exists in the database, is not soft-deleted, and is not archived.
Postconditions:

POST-1. The actor views the novel details, chapter list, ratings, and comments.
Normal Flow:

A. View Novel Details Successfully

  1. Actor accesses a specific novel's detail page.
  2. The system retrieves the novel metadata by slug and verifies its public visibility.
  3. The system retrieves the list of published chapters for the novel ordered by chapter number ascending.
  4. The system retrieves the current actor's session data to determine bookshelf bookmark status, last read chapter, and unlocked VIP chapters.
  5. The system retrieves the novel's favorite count, rating summary, community comments, and other novels by the same author.
  6. The system renders the novel metadata, synopsis, action buttons ("Đọc truyện" or "Đọc tiếp chương X" / "Đọc từ đầu", and "Thêm vào Tủ sách" or "Đã lưu trong Tủ sách"), chapter list with sort toggle ("Cũ → Mới"), same-author recommendations, ratings, and comments.
Alternative Flows:

A.3 Novel Has No Chapters

  1. The system determines there are no published chapters for the novel.
  2. The system disables the reading button ("Chưa có chương") and displays an empty chapter list message.
  3. Back to step 1.

A.6 Toggle Chapter Sort Order

  1. Actor clicks the chapter sort button ("Cũ → Mới" or "Mới → Cũ").
  2. The system reverses the display order of the chapter list on the page.
  3. Back to step 6.
Exceptions:

EX-2 Novel Not Found or Unavailable

  1. The system determines the requested novel slug does not exist, has been soft-deleted, or is archived.
  2. The system throws a resource not found error and displays the 404 error page.
Priority: High, Must Have
Frequency of Use: Very High
Business Rules: BR-03, BR-04, BR-11
Other Information: If an authenticated Member has previously read a chapter of the novel, the primary button displays "Đọc tiếp chương X" alongside a secondary "Đọc từ đầu" button; otherwise, it displays "Đọc truyện".
VIP chapters display a "VIP" badge, coin price, or "Đã mở" status if already unlocked by the Member.
Assumptions:
  • Actor has stable internet connection.
