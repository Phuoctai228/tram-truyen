Category

Rule Definition

BR-01

Constraints

Account Security: Password must be hashed using BCrypt. Reset tokens must have an expiration time.

BR-02

Constraints

Novel Titles: Duplicate novel titles by the same author are prevented.

BR-03

Constraints

Novel Status: ONGOING is the default initial status. The public interface displays non-archived novels (ONGOING, COMPLETED, ON_HOLD) and excludes ARCHIVED novels.

BR-04

Constraints

Novel Deletion: A novel is never physically deleted (soft deletion via is_deleted = true). Soft-deleted and ARCHIVED novels must be strictly excluded from public lists.

BR-05

Constraints

Chapter Management: Only Staff and Admin may create, update, delete (soft delete), lock/unlock, or configure chapters. Chapters with existing purchases cannot be permanently deleted, only soft-deleted (status = archived/hidden).

BR-06

Constraints

VIP Chapters: A VIP chapter must have a positive Coin price. A regular published chapter does not require a Coin price.

BR-07

Constraints

Category Uniqueness: Category name and URL slug must be unique system-wide. Slug must conform to standard URL format.

BR-08

Constraints

Category Deletion: Cannot delete a category if the associated novel count > 0. Admin must detach category from all novels before deletion.

BR-09

Constraints

Category Mapping: Each novel must have a minimum of 1 category and a maximum of 5 categories. Mapping updates must be in a single transaction block.

BR-10

Computations

Category Counts: Publicly displayed novel counts per category only include published novels (ONGOING, COMPLETED).

BR-11

Constraints

Chapter Visibility: Draft or locked chapters are not displayed to Guests/Members. VIP chapters must be visually distinct in the chapter list.

BR-12

Constraints

VIP Access & Audio Reader: Guests and unentitled Members cannot view full VIP chapters (only a preview excerpt is shown), while Staff and Admin have privileged access. Audio playback (Web Speech API) is available to all actors (Guest, Member, Staff, Admin) for any free or unlocked chapter.

BR-13

Computations

Reading Progress: Scroll position is tracked locally in browser localStorage for all actors. Server-side chapter completion (isRead = true) is recorded for authenticated Members when scrolling reaches at least 95% of an unlocked chapter or when short chapter content is fully visible in the viewport. Bookshelf completion percentage is dynamically calculated from read chapters divided by total published chapters.

BR-14

Facts

Reading History: The reading history list is capped at a maximum of 50 recent entries (paginated at 10 items per page), keeping only the latest readAt timestamp per chapter. Removing or clearing history for an unlocked VIP chapter resets its read status (isRead = false, readAt = null) while preserving permanent VIP ownership.

BR-15

Facts

Bookshelf: Bookshelf sorting defaults to the most recently added novel (addedAt DESC, 8 items per page) and supports filtering (All, Reading, Completed), keyword search, and sorting (Recent, Title A-Z, Highest Progress). Removing a novel from the bookshelf only deletes the bookshelf record and preserves the user's reading progress and history.

BR-16

Computations

Leaderboards: Leaderboard updates exclusively count valid view records. Rankings are cached periodically for query optimization.

BR-17

Computations

Ratings: Each Member is restricted to 1 rating record per novel. Subsequent submissions overwrite previous rating. Average rating score is total stars / total reviews, rounded to 1 decimal place.

BR-18

Constraints

Comments: Comment length must be between 5 and 1000 characters. Rate limiting enforced (max 1 comment per 10 seconds per user).

BR-19

Constraints

Hidden Comments: Comments hidden or soft-deleted by Staff/Admin (is_deleted = true) are excluded from public views.

BR-20

Constraints

Transactions: Balance deductions MUST occur within an ACID-compliant transaction using pessimistic locking.

BR-21

Facts

VIP Permanence: Once unlocked, a VIP chapter is permanently accessible to that specific user ID without repurchase.

BR-22

Facts

Daily Check-in: Limited to once per calendar day based on server date. Rewards follow a 7-day consecutive streak cycle (+5, +5, +10, +10, +15, +15, +30 Coins; resets to Day 1 if a day is missed, and cycles back to Day 1 after Day 7). Check-in rewards cannot be withdrawn or converted to fiat currency.

BR-23

Facts

Coin Top-ups: Coin top-up payments are non-refundable and cannot be converted to fiat currency.

BR-24

Facts

Search & Filter: Search is case-insensitive. Selecting multiple categories applies combination logic. Pagination state and filters are preserved during sorting.
